TonyTong 當選 HKDCA Web3 孵化教育委員會主席

HKDCA-HKBA PR · 2022/6/17 · 中文報導 · Nasdaq PR

Hong Kong Digital Currency Academy (HKDCA) Launches Web3 & Blockchain Education in partnership with Hong Kong Blockchain Association (HKBA) Com2000 Web3 Incubator.

Hong Kong Digital Currency Academy HKDCA and World Digital Currency Forum (WDCF) launched on June 16 with over 1000 participants at Kerry Hotel Hong Kong, themed "Web3 Era, New Technology, New Vision." The launch ceremony presented HKBA Com2000 Web3Hub Co-Chairman TonyTong as Web3 Incubation & Education Committee Chairman of Hong Kong Digital Currency Academy.

HKBA.club held the Com2000 Web3 Incubation Panel "The Future of Web3, Fintech, Metaverse" with HKBA Co-Chairman TonyTong (Com2000), Dr. Lo Wai Shun (HKBA Co-Chairman, DLCapitals.com), Mr. Sam Lee (CoinStreet, Web3A.org), Mr. Victor Lee (AsiaMeta, Starmount Venture), Mr. Derek Leung (HKBA Com2000 Web3 Advisor) and Mr. Phillip Wong (HKBA AsiaMeta Incubation Fund).
